Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how TimeStamp Camera – GPS Date & Location (“the App”) handles information on your device. The App is developed and published by Ajith Kumar Gokulan (“Developer”, “we”, “our”, “us”).

1. Information We Collect

The App does not create user accounts and does not store personal data on our servers. All data remains on your device. The App uses the following permissions only for core functionality:

  • Camera – to capture photos and record videos.
  • Microphone – to record audio together with video.
  • Location (GPS) – to obtain latitude, longitude and address for overlays and map links.
  • Storage / Media – to save and read photos and videos on your device.

2. How We Use Information

All information accessed through permissions is used only to:

  • Display live overlay text (date, time, GPS, address, compass, index, version, contact)
  • Save photos and videos with overlay to your device
  • Create a Google Maps link in the overlay text
  • Allow you to copy overlay text to clipboard

We do not use your data for analytics, advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Photos and videos are stored locally on your device using the standard Android media storage. We do not have access to your media. You can delete any photo or video at any time using your gallery or file manager.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party libraries and services such as:

  • Google Play Services / Location Services
  • FFmpeg/FFmpegKit library

These services may collect technical data as described in their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

We do not transmit your media to our own servers. Protecting your device (PIN, password, fingerprint) and deciding how you share your media is your responsibility.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
